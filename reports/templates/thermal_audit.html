5. Statutory Defensibility & Limitations (TRD Compliance)

Mandatory Methodological Disclosures:
1. LST is not Air Temperature (FR-60): Land Surface Temperature (LST) measures radiative skin temperature. Nocturnal LST is a validated proxy for nocturnal thermal stress.
2. Physics Constraints Enforced: All estimates are derived via LightGBM models enforcing monotone constraints (increasing vegetation mathematically cannot predict warming).
3. Weather Normalised: Macro-meteorological heatwave anomalies from ECMWF ERA5-Land have been normalised to isolate the urban-form signal.
4. Data Sources: NASA MODIS Terra (MOD11A1), ESA WorldCover 10m, Copernicus Sentinel-2 MSI, JRC GHSL Built Height, NOAA VIIRS DNB.
